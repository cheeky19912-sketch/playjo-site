# Privacy Policy – Play Jo

**Effective date:** 21 / 11 / 2026  ·  **Last updated:** 21 / 11 / 2026

## 1. Who we are
**Play Jo** (the "App", "we", "us") is operated by **Al-Abed Company (شركة العابد)**, registered in the Hashemite Kingdom of Jordan under commercial registration no. **77630**, address 7th Circle, Amman, Jordan. We are the "controller" of your personal data within the meaning of Jordan's **Personal Data Protection Law No. 24 of 2023** ("PDPL").

Privacy contact: **info@playjo.app**.

## 2. Scope
This policy covers the Play Jo app on iOS and Android, the website **playjo.app**, and our official social accounts (e.g., Instagram **@playjo.official**). It applies to all users: players, team captains, court owners, referees and platform administrators.

## 3. Data we collect

### a) Data you provide
| Data | Required? | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Jordanian mobile number | Yes | Account creation, login via one-time code (OTP), booking communication |
| First and last name | Yes | Identifying you in bookings, teams and line-ups |
| Confirmation that you are 18+ | Yes | Eligibility |
| Date of birth | Optional | Age verification, personalisation |
| Address | Optional | Suggesting nearby courts |
| Email | Optional | Receipts and important notices |
| Names and phone numbers of teammates you invite | When inviting | Inviting them to your team (see 3(d)) |
| Referee application (experience, areas) | Referees only | Reviewing applications, assigning matches |
| Court details (name, location, photos, prices, hours) | Court owners only | Listing the court, managing bookings |
| Payout details: CliQ alias or Jordanian IBAN and bank name | Court owners & referees only | Paying out your earnings |
| Match reports (score, goals, cards, MVP, notes) | Referees | Publishing tournament results |
| Photos and videos you upload | Optional | Publishing on the match page after admin approval |

### b) Data generated when you use the App
- **Bookings and participation:** court, date, time, amount, booking reference, payment status, teams and tournaments you join.
- **Payment records:** amount, method, CliQ transfer reference, confirmation status. **We do not store card numbers or CVV**; these are processed directly by the payment gateway.
- **Notifications:** your device push token and the notifications sent to you.
- **Technical data:** device type and OS, device language, error and security logs (e.g., login time).

### c) Data we do not collect
We do not collect precise location (GPS), your phone contacts, or use third-party advertising trackers.

### d) Data about other people
When you invite a player by name and phone number, you confirm you have their permission to share their number with us for that purpose. The number is visible only to members of that team and to platform admins, and is deleted if the person does not join within 30 days after the tournament ends.

## 4. Legal bases
We process your data on one or more of the following bases under the PDPL:
- **Providing the service you requested** (bookings, tournament registration, payouts).
- **Your consent** (optional data, marketing notifications, uploads). You may withdraw consent at any time without affecting prior lawful processing.
- **Legal obligation** (accounting and tax records, lawful requests from authorities).
- **Legitimate interest** (fraud prevention, platform security, service improvement), where it does not override your rights.

## 5. How we use your data
1. Creating your account and verifying you by OTP.
2. Handling court bookings and running tournaments, teams and matches.
3. Receiving and confirming payments, splitting payments among players, and issuing refunds.
4. Paying court owners and referees.
5. Sending service notifications (booking confirmation, match reminders, results, payment status).
6. Publishing tournament results, statistics and media (see section 7).
7. Customer support and dispute resolution.
8. Detecting fraud and abuse and protecting the platform.
9. Complying with Jordanian law.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not make solely automated decisions that significantly affect you.

## 6. Who we share data with
| Recipient | What they see | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Court owner** | Your name, booking reference, time slot, payment status | Preparing the court and receiving you |
| **Your teammates** | Your name, phone number and payment status of your share | Team coordination |
| **Assigned referee** | Names of both teams' players | Recording goals and cards |
| **All users** | Team name, player names in line-ups, results and stats, MVP, approved media | Displaying the tournament |
| **Service providers (processors)** | Only what is needed | Database and storage hosting (Supabase), OTP SMS, payment gateway, push notifications (Apple, Google/Expo) |
| **Banks and the CliQ system** | Transfer details | Executing payments and payouts |
| **Authorities** | As required by law | Binding legal requests |
| **Sponsors** | No personal data; only their logos appear in the App | — |

All service providers are contractually bound to protect your data and use it only on our instructions.

## 7. Photos, videos and Instagram
- Matches in Play Jo tournaments may be photographed and filmed, and media may be published in the App and on our official accounts (e.g., **@playjo.official**).
- By registering for a tournament you agree to appear in such media.
- You may ask us at any time to remove media in which you are clearly identifiable via **info@playjo.app**; we will remove it from the App and our accounts within 7 business days. We cannot control copies saved or reshared by others.
- User-uploaded media is published only after admin review.

## 8. International transfers
Your data may be stored on our providers' servers outside Jordan (e.g., Supabase servers). Such transfers are made under the conditions set by the PDPL and its implementing regulations and instructions, and we select providers that apply adequate protection.

## 9. Retention
| Data | Period |
|---|---|
| Account data | While your account is active; deleted within 30 days of an account deletion request |
| Booking, payment and payout records | 7 years or as required by tax and accounting law |
| Tournament results and stats | Kept as the tournament's historical record; your name can be anonymised when you delete your account |
| Phone numbers of invited players who never joined | 30 days after the tournament ends |
| OTP codes | A few minutes, until expiry |
| Security logs | 12 months |

## 10. Your rights
Under the PDPL you have the right to:
- **Be informed and access** the data we process about you and obtain a copy.
- **Rectify** or update your data (most fields can be edited in Settings).
- **Erasure** of your data, subject to what we must keep by law.
- **Restrict or object** to processing.
- **Withdraw consent** at any time for consent-based processing.
- **Lodge a complaint** with Jordan's competent data protection authority (the Personal Data Protection Council).

To exercise your rights, email **info@playjo.app**. We may verify your identity via your registered phone number and will respond within 30 days or the period set by law.

### Account deletion
You can delete your account in the App under **Settings → Delete account**, or via the [account deletion page](delete-account.html). An account with an upcoming paid booking or unsettled payouts can be deleted once these are settled.

## 11. Children
The App is for users **18 and over**. We do not knowingly collect data from minors and will delete any such account we discover. If you are a parent and believe your child has registered, contact us.

## 12. Security
We apply appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your data. If a breach is likely to affect your data, we will notify the competent authority and you as required by law and without undue delay.

## 13. Notifications
You can turn off push notifications in your phone or App settings. Service-critical notices (e.g., payment confirmation) will still appear inside the App.

## 14. Changes
We may update this policy. We will notify you in the App at least 14 days before any material change takes effect and update the "Last updated" date.

## 15. Language
This policy is available in Arabic and English. In case of conflict, the **Arabic text prevails**.
