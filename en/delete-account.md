# Delete your Play Jo account

You can delete your account and personal data at any time.

## In the app
Open the app → **Settings** → **Delete account**, then confirm with the code sent to your phone.

## Without the app
Email **info@playjo.app** or WhatsApp **+962 79 133 3155** from your registered phone number with: "Please delete my Play Jo account". We will verify you with a code sent to your number and complete the request within 30 days.

## What is deleted
- Your phone number, name, date of birth, address and email.
- Payout details (CliQ / IBAN).
- Photos and videos you uploaded.
- Your device's push notification token.

## What we keep and why
- **Payment, booking and payout records:** kept for 7 years because accounting and tax laws require it, and used for nothing else.
- **Tournament results:** results and stats remain as the tournament's record, but your name is replaced with "Former player".

## Before deletion
If you have an upcoming paid booking or unpaid earnings, we settle them first (cancelling the booking under the **Payment & Refund Policy**, or paying out your earnings), then delete the account.

See the [Privacy Policy](privacy.html) for more details.
